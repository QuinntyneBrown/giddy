# 09 · Choose a theme

A theme ties a party together, from the invitation to the cake. This video shows how Giddy suggests themes that suit the child, how a parent narrows them, and how choosing one takes a single tap and can be undone.

## What themes promise

Themes start from the child. For Zuri, who's turning seven, only themes for her age are shown at first, and the ones that match what Giddy remembers about her are tagged. Filters update straight away and say whether they're on. And whatever theme is chosen, the invitation and Fun and food follow it.

## Browsing themes

Here are the party themes on a laptop. Eleven of the thirteen suit Zuri's age, and Dino Dig is already chosen for her. Mad Science Lab is tagged Zuri might love, because Giddy remembers she loves science. Turning on Outdoor leaves the outdoor themes. Turning off the For Zuri filter and choosing ages three to five shows themes for younger children, and switching the filters back brings everything back.

## When nothing matches

If no theme fits the filters, they stay where they are, and Giddy offers to clear them or to ask Giddy for an idea instead.

## Choosing a theme

On a phone, Amara chooses Space Explorers. The tile says chosen for Zuri, the list stays where she was, and a toast confirms that Giddy will use it for invites, the cake and activity ideas. For eight seconds the toast offers Undo, and Undo puts Dino Dig back.

## How it is tested

The API tests cover the child's age filter, the age band, outdoor and budget filters, tags from Giddy's memories, choosing and undoing, clearing the theme, the invitation and Fun and food following the theme, and that viewers can't change it. The browser tests drive the same flows against the fake API at phone and desktop widths, with an accessibility check on the page.

## Recap

Things to remember. Themes start from the child's age and interests. Filters stay visible and say whether they're on. Choosing a theme keeps your place and can be undone for eight seconds. Next, parents manage the budget.
