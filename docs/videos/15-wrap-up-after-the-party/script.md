# 15 · Wrap up after the party

The party is over, the cake is gone, and there's one last job: saying thank you. This video shows Giddy's wrap-up, with thank-you notes drafted for every guest, ratings for the businesses you booked, the final numbers, and a plan saved for next year.

## When wrap-up opens

Wrap-up opens the day after the party. Before then, the Wrap-up tab says what will be waiting: thank-you notes drafted by Giddy for every guest, reviews of the venue and vendors, and a quick way to reuse the plan next year.

## Thank-you notes

Here's Amara, the day after Zuri's mermaid party. Giddy has drafted fourteen thank-you notes, one for each guest who came, written in Zuri's voice and mentioning the gift Amara logged. Leo's note doesn't mention a gift yet, so she opens it and adds the T. rex plush he brought, and Giddy rewrites the draft to thank him for it. If she changes the wording herself, Giddy keeps her words. Ava's family has no email on file, so her note is marked No email and won't be sent. Then she sends the other thirteen at once. Each family gets the note by email, every note shows as Sent, and sending again never sends a note twice.

## Ratings and final numbers

On her phone, Amara rates the businesses she booked through Giddy. Only the venue and vendors with a confirmed booking or an accepted quote can be rated. She gives the pool five stars, and they count in its rating for other parents. A written review is optional, and she adds one. The final numbers show fourteen of sixteen kids came, seven hundred and two dollars spent against a budget of seven hundred and twenty, and twelve gifts logged.

## Do it again next year

Save as template keeps the theme, the venue and vendors, and the checklist. Giddy will remind Amara about eight weeks before Zuri's next birthday, and Start a party from it opens a new party with the same plan, the vendors ready to book again and the checklist moved to the new date. On a past party's overview, Reuse this plan does the same in one step.

## How it is tested

The API tests cover when wrap-up opens, drafts for guests who came, gifts and edited notes, sending once by email, ratings that count in the public rating and only for booked businesses, final numbers, saving the plan, starting a party from it, the reminder, and who can see and change what. The browser tests drive the page and both dialogs against the fake API, including viewers and an emptied note, with accessibility checks at phone and desktop widths.

## Recap

Things to remember. Giddy drafts the thank-yous, and the parent has the last word. Only real bookings get rated. The final numbers close the loop on the budget. And this year's plan becomes next year's head start. That's the whole journey, from the first idea to the last thank-you.
