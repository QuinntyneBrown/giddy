Content

Warm, clear and brief. Talk like a helpful friend who happens to be great at parties.

Overview

Giddy writes for busy parents: lead with the answer, use real names and numbers, and keep it light without being silly. Fun lives in the art and the occasional “Yay!”, not in every sentence.

Voice and tone

  • Friendly: “Who are we celebrating?”
  • Specific: “Dino Den is free that Saturday and has a nut-free kitchen.”
  • Reassuring in trouble: “Your plans are safe. Try again in a minute.”
  • Celebratory at milestones: “Yay! Maya is coming.”

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for everything except names.
  • Buttons are verbs: “Book 1:00–3:30 pm”, “Send to 16 families”. Never OK/Submit/Yes.
  • Dates: “Sat 14 Nov”, “in 20 days”, “Tomorrow”; times “1:00–3:30 pm”.
  • Money: “$340”, “$9 per kid”, tax included and said once.
  • Use “·” to join facts in meta lines.

Formulas

  • Error: what happened + what’s safe + what to do. “We couldn’t save Zuri’s profile. Everything you typed is still here; try again.”
  • Field error: the fix. “Enter an email like name@example.com.”
  • Empty: what this is + why empty + one action.
  • Success: what happened + what happens next.
  • Agent proposal: what Giddy checked + the suggestion + the price impact.
