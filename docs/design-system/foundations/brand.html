Brand

Giddy is an agentic birthday planner for families in Toronto and the GTA. This page covers how Giddy looks and sounds, so every screen, invitation and email feels like it came from the same friend.

The idea

Giddy is the fizzy, can’t-sit-still feeling a kid has on the morning of their birthday. Our job is to give parents that feeling instead of the to-do list.

Calm in the planning
Parents arrive stressed. Screens stay quiet, clear and cream so the decisions feel small.
Joy in the party
Colour, confetti and the mascot show up at the milestones: a booking, an RSVP, the big day.
Specific beats cute
A real venue, a real time, a real price is more delightful than any exclamation mark.

Logo

Two pieces: the wordmark leads, the balloon symbol stands in when space is tight. Construction, colourways, clear space, minimum sizes and misuse are on the Logo page.

Grape leads, cream gives it room to breathe, and the party pops are confetti: a little goes a long way. Full ramps and semantic roles are on the Color page.

Giddy the balloon

Giddy has four moods, and each one has a job. Never invent a fifth. The artwork and its tokens are on the Mascot page.

Giddy is an “it” and is always called by name. Label the art for screen readers (“Giddy looking worried”), or hide it when the text beside it says the same.

Voice

A helpful friend who happens to be great at parties. Lead with the answer, use real names and numbers, and save the fun for the art. The full content rules are on the Content page.

Voice: what we say and what we avoid
When we’reWe sayNot
Friendly✓ Who are we celebrating?✕ Please enter child profile details.
Specific✓ Dino Den is free that Saturday and has a nut-free kitchen.✕ We found amazing options for your little one!!
Reassuring✓ Your plans are safe. Try again in a minute.✕ Oops! Something went terribly wrong.
Celebratory✓ Yay! Ethan is coming.✕ RSVP status updated to: Attending.

Buttons are verbs with the specifics in them: “Book 1:00–3:30 pm”, “Send to 16 families”. Never OK or Submit.
