Foundation

Logo

Two pieces. The giddy wordmark leads; the balloon symbol stands in where the wordmark won’t fit. The app icon is the symbol on a grape tile.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSource: Magic Patterns “Giddy Logo & Wordmark”

Wordmark

The primary logo, and the default on every surface that has room for it: the app’s top bar and sidebar, the marketing site, emails and invitations.

Party planning that feels like a party.

Construction

Ascenderx-heightBaselineDescender
  • Every letter is one 13-unit round stroke. The bowls of the g and d are perfect circles and every terminal is round, so the word reads soft at any size.
  • The dot of the i is a balloon that has just let go. It is the only colour in the wordmark and the only part allowed to tilt toward joy.
  • Lowercase, always. Giddy is a friend, not an institution. In running text the name is still written “Giddy”.
  • The artwork is 272 × 132 units (viewBox="0 4 272 132"); width always follows height.

Symbol and app icon

The smiling balloon. Use it where the wordmark won’t fit: avatars, favicons, the chat agent. The face drops below 24px, where it turns to noise. The expressive, many-mood balloon is the mascot.

App icon

Cream balloon on a grape tile with a 22.5% corner radius. Shipped as assets/brand/app-icon.svg (with face, 32px and up; the 180px touch icon is rendered square, because iOS rounds it) and assets/brand/favicon.svg (no face: the browser tab).

Colourways

Four approved versions. Pick by background, never by mood. In the product the wordmark reads --color-logo-ink and --color-logo-tittle, so it is Primary in the light theme and Night in the dark theme without any extra class.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin equal to the balloon’s height, x, on every side. Nothing else (text, icons, edges) enters it.

Misuse

Accessibility

  • The wordmark is an image named “Giddy” (role="img" aria-label="Giddy"). Inside a link it gives the link its name, so the top bar’s brand link reads “Giddy”, or the link’s own label such as “Giddy, back to the top”.
  • A logotype is exempt from WCAG 1.4.3, but the letters are still held to 3:1 against the surface they sit on (8.5:1 light, 16.7:1 dark).
  • In forced-colours mode the letters and the balloon take CanvasText.
  • Decorative copies of the symbol beside a visible “Giddy” are aria-hidden.
