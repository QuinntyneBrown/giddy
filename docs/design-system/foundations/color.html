Foundation

Color

A grape accent on a warm cream canvas, six “party pop” colours for fun, and a night-sky dark theme. Every text pairing passes WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Colour has three jobs in Giddy: brand (grape, the accent and Giddy’s balloon), meaning (info, success, warning, danger) and joy (sun, pink, mint, sky, orange and grape pops on tiles, avatars and confetti). Pops are decorative and always paired with words; status colours carry meaning and always come with an icon and text.

  • Light: cream-50 canvas, white surfaces, grape-600 accent.
  • Dark: night-950 canvas, night-900 surfaces, night-850 raised, grape-300 accent with dark text on it.
  • Never write a colour in a component; use a semantic token.

Usage

  • One accent per view: the primary button and the current nav item.
  • Pops are decoration; pair them with text.
  • New brand theme: add [data-theme="name"] overriding semantic tokens only, then extend contrast-pairs.json and rerun the checker.
