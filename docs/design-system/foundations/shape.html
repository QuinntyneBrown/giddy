Overview

Radius grows with size: 8px compact, 14px inputs, 20px cards and dialogs, 28px sheets and heroes, full pills for buttons, chips, badges and avatars.

Radii

--radius-xs
--radius-sm
--radius-md
--radius-lg
--radius-xl
--radius-full

Borders

  • 1px hairline for edges and dividers.
  • 2px for choice cards, focus rings and the dashed plan card.

Usage

  • Buttons are always pills.
  • Nested shapes use a smaller radius than their container.
