Pattern

Forms

One column, visible labels, errors on submit with a summary, and never lose what a parent typed.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Forms collect party basics, guests, RSVPs and settings. They are short, pre-filled from what Giddy already knows, and forgiving.

When to use

SituationUse
2–6 fields, task-focusedForm in a card or dialog
Multi-step setupWizard with stepper
One setting that applies nowSwitch, no Save
Picture-first choicesChoice cards
DestructiveConfirmation dialog, typed name for big losses

States

StateWhat happens
DefaultFirst field focused when the form is the page’s purpose
InvalidSummary at top gets focus; inline errors; aria-invalid
SubmittingPrimary busy, fields read-only, no layout shift
FailedDanger alert above the form; inputs kept; Try again
SuccessSay what happened and what’s next; or move on

Responsive

  • One column on phones; related pairs side by side from 768px.
  • Primary full width at the bottom on phones.

Accessibility

  • Labels on every control; errors linked with aria-describedby.
  • Summary links jump to fields.
  • autocomplete on personal fields.
  • No time limits.

Content

  • Submit says the result: “Send to 16 families”.
  • Errors start with the fix.
