Overview

See the content foundation for the full rules. This pattern shows them applied.

When to use

Instead ofWrite
SubmitSend my reply

Structure

Giddy: Here’s a plan that keeps you $22 under budget and everything is within 10 minutes of home.

Agent copy: numbers first, then the action.

States

MomentTone
PlanningFriendly, efficient
SuccessCelebratory, brief
ProblemsCalm, reassuring, specific
Guests’ parentsPolite, minimal

Responsive

  • Copy must fit at 360px; buttons never wrap.

Accessibility

  • Link text makes sense alone.
  • Emoji never replace words.

Content

  • Sentence case; “·” between facts; dates like “Sat 14 Nov”.
