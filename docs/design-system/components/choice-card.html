Choice card

Choice cards turn radios and checkboxes into big friendly tiles with an emoji: interests, time of day, invitation design, “Yes, Maya’s coming!”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use when the options are visual or fun and there are 2–10 of them. Each card is a real <input> stretched over the tile, so keyboard and screen readers behave exactly like a radio or checkbox.

Anatomy

  1. Tile2px border, --radius-lg; selected = accent border + subtle fill.
  2. ArtEmoji or avatar, decorative.
  3. LabelShort, with optional caption.

Variants

Multi-select interests (checkbox) and single-select RSVP (radio).
HTML
<div class="choice-cards" style="width: 100%;"><label class="choice-card"><input type="checkbox" checked><span class="emoji" aria-hidden="true">🦕</span><span>Dinosaurs</span></label><label class="choice-card"><input type="checkbox" checked><span class="emoji" aria-hidden="true">🔬</span><span>Science</span></label><label class="choice-card"><input type="checkbox"><span class="emoji" aria-hidden="true">🚀</span><span>Space</span></label><label class="choice-card pop--mint"><input type="radio" name="vcc" checked><span class="emoji" aria-hidden="true">🎉</span><span>Yes, coming!</span></label></div>

Sizes

Not applicable: Cards are fluid: minimum 9rem wide, auto-fill.

States

VariantDefaultFocusCheckedDisabled
Card

Responsive behaviour

  • Auto-fill grid, min 9rem: 2 per row at 360px, 3–5 on wider screens.

Theming

Selected uses --color-accent-subtle and --color-fg-accent; add .pop--* to tint a card for RSVP or invitation designs.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radio or checkbox inside a <label>, visually hidden by opacity but covering the card.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the group.
SpaceToggles (checkbox).
ArrowsMove selection (radio).

Focus

Ring on the card via :has(input:focus-visible).

Labelling

Card text is the label; emoji are aria-hidden. Groups need a legend.

Contrast

Selected label
Selected edge

Motion

Colour and border changes use --duration-fast (120 ms) with --ease-standard; reduced motion zeroes the duration in the tokens, so nothing extra is needed.

Touch

Whole card is the target, well above 44px.

Content

  • One or two words plus an optional caption.
  • Emoji support the word, never replace it.
