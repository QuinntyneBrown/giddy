Alert & banner

Alerts call out something on the page: an overdue task, a declined payment, a transit closure on party day.

Overview

Inline alerts sit in content; banners (.banner) span the page under the top bar (offline, verify email). Only new urgent content uses role="alert".

Anatomy

  1. IconTone.
  2. TitleLabel weight.
  3. TextWhat to do.
  4. ActionsLinks or small buttons.
  5. DismissOptional.

Responsive behaviour

Theming

Each tone sets --alert-bg/-fg/-border/-icon from status tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert" for new urgent problems, role="status" for updates, none for static notes.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only.

Icon decorative; title carries the tone in words.
