Component · Giddy

Mascot & logo

Giddy is a smiling grape balloon. The small mark is the logo; the mascot shows moods: happy, party, sleepy and worried.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The mascot is inline SVG coloured by tokens, so it themes automatically. Use the mood that fits: happy in chat and greetings, party (hat) for success and first-run, sleepy (sky) for empty/no results/404, worried (orange) for errors.

Anatomy

  1. Logo mark + wordmarkDisplay font, heavy, accent.
  2. MascotBalloon, cheeks, face, knot, string.

Variants

Happy, party, sleepy, worried.

Mascot

Responsive behaviour

  • xl on landing and status pages; lg in empty states; sm in chat.

Theming

Balloon --color-accent, cheeks pink pop, face --color-fg-on-accent. Moods swap the balloon to orange or sky pop.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<svg role="img" aria-label> when meaningful; aria-hidden when next to text that says the same.
