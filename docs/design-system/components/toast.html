Toast

Toasts confirm what just happened (“Ethan Kim removed”) and offer Undo, then get out of the way.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Toasts sit bottom-right (bottom, full width, above the tab bar on phones). Success/info auto-dismiss after 8 s and pause on hover/focus; warnings stay 12 s; errors stay until dismissed. Max 3 visible.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone colour.
  2. Title + textWhat happened.
  3. ActionUndo / View / Fix.
  4. DismissLabelled.
  5. TimerShows time left.

Variants

Reminder scheduled
Professor Fizz hasn’t confirmed
2 reminders didn’t send
Info, warning, danger (persistent).

Responsive behaviour

  • < 640px: full width, above the bottom nav.
  • ≥ 640px: 24rem, bottom-right.

Theming

Inverse surface: dark toast on light, light toast on dark. Icons use status icon tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Region role="status" aria-live="polite" exists before toasts arrive; errors use role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action and dismiss.
EscDismisses the focused toast.

Focus

Toasts never take focus.

Labelling

Dismiss: “Dismiss”.

Text

Motion

Rises in over --duration-base; fades out.

Action and dismiss ≥ 24px.

Content

  • Title in past tense: “Reminder sent to 3 families”.
  • Offer Undo when reversible.
