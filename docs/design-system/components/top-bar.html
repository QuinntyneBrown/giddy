Top bar

The top bar stays at the top of every signed-in screen with search, notifications and the account avatar.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

64px tall (--layout-topbar-height), sticky, on the surface colour. On phones it shows the Giddy wordmark (the sidebar is hidden); from 768px it adds search; from 1024px the wordmark moves to the sidebar. Public pages use a simpler bar with Sign in and the primary “Start planning free”.

Anatomy

  1. WordmarkLogo mark + “Giddy” in display type; phones only in the app.
  2. SearchFrom 768px.
  3. NotificationsIcon link with count badge; name includes the count.
  4. AccountAvatar link to settings.

Responsive behaviour

  • < 768px: wordmark, bell, avatar. Navigation lives in the bottom nav.
  • 768–1023px: adds search.
  • ≥ 1024px: wordmark moves into the sidebar.

Theming

Surface background with a default border; dark uses night-900.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header> landmark containing a role="search" form.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only. The sticky bar never covers focused content: pages set scroll-padding-top to its height.

Labelling

Bell: “Notifications, 3 unread”. Avatar: “Account settings for Amara Okafor”.

Wordmark

Motion

None.

Touch

Icon buttons 40px, avatar 32px inside a 40px hit area.

Content

  • Search placeholder hints scope: “Search venues, entertainers, cakes…”.
