Breadcrumb

Breadcrumbs show the way back up: My parties, then the party, then the section.

Overview

Used on party pages, venue detail and compare. Not on top-level pages.

Anatomy

  1. Ancestor linksMuted, underline on hover.
  2. Separator“/”, decorative.
  3. Current pageDefault colour, not a link.

Variants

  1. Venues
  2. Dino Den Play Café
Two levels.
HTML
<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb example"><ol class="breadcrumb"><li><a href="#variants">Venues</a></li><li aria-current="page">Dino Den Play Café</li></ol></nav>

Role and pattern

KeyAction
