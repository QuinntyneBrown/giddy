Card

Cards are the soft white (or night-purple) surfaces everything sits on, with generous 20px corners.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use a card to group a section (Giddy’s nudges, RSVPs, Budget). Interactive cards (party rows, tiles) are a single link; the card lifts 1px on hover. Selected cards get an accent ring. Tint a card with .pop--* for kid-friendly emphasis (the RSVP tally, “Coming up next”).

Anatomy

  1. HeaderTitle + optional action.
  2. BodyAny content.
  3. FooterActions, top divider.
  4. Surface--card-* tokens, --shadow-1.

Responsive behaviour

  • Padding stays 24px; grids reflow from 1 to 3 columns.
  • Cards never scroll horizontally.

Theming

Override --card-bg, --card-border, --card-radius, --card-padding. Dark cards are night-900 on a night-950 canvas, with a lighter border so edges stay visible.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain container; an interactive card is one <a> or <button>, never a div with a click handler.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only. on the whole card.

Labelling

A section card has a heading; interactive cards are named by their title.

Content
