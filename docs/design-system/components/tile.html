Tile

Tiles are the colourful cards for themes, venues and vendors: big emoji art on a pop colour, then name, facts and price.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Tiles are what makes Giddy feel like a toy box. The art area is a pop colour with a large emoji (real photos replace it when venues upload them). Availability or tags sit on the art as badges; a favourite button sits top right.

Anatomy

  1. Art16:10, pop background, emoji at --font-size-5xl.
  2. BadgeOn a surface pill.
  3. TitleH4 size.
  4. FooterPrice + rating or action.
