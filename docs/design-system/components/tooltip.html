Tooltip

Tooltips name icon-only buttons such as the notification bell.

Overview

Tooltips repeat the accessible name of an icon-only control. They never hold anything interactive or essential.

Anatomy

  1. BubbleInverse surface, caption text.
  2. ArrowPoints at the trigger.

Variants

Text only.
