Progress

Progress shows how far along something is: the checklist bar, an import, or the “Plan 68% ready” ring.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Determinate when the value is known; indeterminate otherwise. The ring is Giddy’s summary of plan readiness on the hero and overview.

Anatomy

  1. RingConic accent arc around a value.
  2. Bar8px track, accent fill.

Variants

68%
Determinate, indeterminate, success, ring on hero.
HTML
<div class="stack" style="width: min(100%, 20rem);"><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Import" aria-valuenow="60" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 60%;"></div></div><div class="progress progress--indeterminate" role="progressbar" aria-label="Checking availability"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div><div class="progress progress--success" role="progressbar" aria-label="Done" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 100%;"></div></div></div><div class="hero" style="padding: var(--space-4);"><div class="ring" style="--value: 68;" role="img" aria-label="Plan 68% ready">68%</div></div>

Sizes

Not applicable: Bar 8px; ring 72px.

States

VariantDefaultLoading
Bar
68%

Responsive behaviour

  • Bars fill their container.

Theming

Track --color-bg-subtle-hover, fill --color-accent; on hero the ring switches to white arcs.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max and a label; ring is role="img" with the value in its label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not interactive.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

“Checklist 30% done”.

Contrast

Fill vs track
Ring on hero

Motion

Width eases over --duration-slow; indeterminate loops (--duration-loop) and becomes static with reduced motion.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Pair with words: “7 of 10 steps done”.
