Component · Giddy

Video

The video plays a short narrated clip, such as the marketing site’s twenty-second pitch, with the browser’s own controls, a poster, captions the viewer turns on and a transcript anyone can read.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use the video for a short, narrated clip that sells or explains, never for content the page depends on: everything it says is also in the transcript. It never autoplays and never loops. The library component is figure[giddy-video] in @giddy/components.

Anatomy

  1. Frame.video__frame: 16:9, extra-large radius, raised shadow, sunken background while the poster loads.
  2. Native controlsThe browser’s play, progress, volume, captions and full-screen controls. The captions track is English only and off until the viewer turns it on.
  3. Transcript.video__transcript: a disclosure with the narration, closed by default.

Variants

Default: poster, controls, captions track, transcript closed.
HTML
<figure class="video"><div class="video__frame"><video controls playsinline preload="none" poster="../../mocks/assets/marketing/meet-giddy.jpg" aria-label="Meet Giddy, a twenty-second tour" width="1920" height="1080"><track kind="captions" srclang="en" label="English" src="../../mocks/assets/marketing/meet-giddy.vtt"></video></div><details class="video__transcript"><summary>Read the transcript</summary><div class="video__transcript-body"><p>Planning a kid’s party in the GTA?</p><p>Just tell Giddy about your kid, and get real venues, fun and cake near you, in one priced plan.</p><p>Then invites, RSVPs and a run sheet for the day.</p><p>Start planning free.</p></div></details></figure>
Unavailable: the browser cannot play the file. The poster stays as a still, a status line says so, and the transcript opens.

Sizes

Not applicable: The video fills its container at 16:9; the page sets the width (the marketing site caps it at 56rem).

States

StateWhat shows
ReadyPoster and controls; nothing downloads beyond metadata (preload="metadata").
PlayingThe browser’s controls; captions only when the viewer chose them.
UnavailablePoster as an image, .video__fallback status with a link to the transcript, transcript open.
Transcript opendetails[open]; the plus turns into a cross.

Responsive behaviour

  • Always 16:9 and full width of its container; no horizontal scroll at 360 px.
  • Below 640 px the radius drops to large, and the unavailable message moves under the poster instead of floating over it.
  • playsinline keeps phones from forcing full screen on play.

Theming

The frame uses --color-bg-surface-sunken and --shadow-3; the fallback and transcript use surface and border roles, so both themes follow automatically. The video picture itself does not change with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <video controls> (no custom player) inside a <figure>; the transcript is a native <details> disclosure (WAI-ARIA disclosure pattern).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the player’s controls, then the transcript summary.
Space / EnterPlays or pauses on the player; opens or closes the transcript on its summary.

Focus

The shared ring on the transcript summary and fallback link; the browser draws focus inside the native controls.

Labelling

The video has an aria-label naming the clip (“Meet Giddy, a twenty-second tour”). Exactly one <track kind="captions" srclang="en" label="English">, without default, so captions stay off until turned on (WCAG 1.2.2). The transcript is the text alternative for the audio and the visuals (1.2.1, 1.2.3). The unavailable message is a role="status".

Transcript text
Fallback text

Motion

Never autoplays, never loops, and pauses on the viewer’s say, so WCAG 2.2.2 holds. With reduced motion the transcript icon does not rotate.

Touch

The transcript summary is at least 44 px tall; the native controls follow the platform.

Content

  • Name the clip and its length in the label: “Meet Giddy, a twenty-second tour”.
  • The transcript is the narration word for word, one paragraph per sentence group.
  • Fallback: “This browser can’t play the video.” plus “Read the transcript”.
