Form layout

Form layout arranges fields in one column, puts an error summary on top and keeps the actions at the end.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Forms are one column, inside a card or dialog. Validate on submit (then live as people fix): show the .form-summary alert at the top linking to each field, and move focus to it. While submitting, the primary is busy and fields are read-only.

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryDanger alert with links to fields.
  2. Fields16px apart.
  3. ActionsRight-aligned; primary last.

Variants

Two columns only for short related fields (≥ 768px).
HTML
<div class="grid-2" style="width: 100%;"><div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="fl2">First name</label><input class="input" id="fl2" value="Amara"></div><div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="fl3">Last name</label><input class="input" id="fl3" value="Okafor"></div></div>

Sizes

Not applicable: Forms fill their container; prose forms cap at 40rem (<code>.wizard</code>).

States

VariantDefaultInvalidLoading
Actions2 things to fix

Responsive behaviour

  • One column under 768px.
  • Actions wrap; on phones the primary goes full width at the bottom.
  • Wizards use a stepper; only the current label shows under 768px.

Theming

Composes field, alert and button tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<form novalidate> with custom messages; summary is role="alert" and receives focus on submit.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EnterSubmits from a single-line field.
TabMoves through fields in visual order.

Focus

Summary on failed submit; the first field on load for task-first forms (sign-in).

Labelling

Every group has a legend; the submit says the outcome.

Contrast

Summary text

Motion

None.

Touch

Primary ≥ 44px; full width on phones.

Content

  • Summary title is friendly: “Let’s fix a couple of things”.
  • Never clear what people typed.

</form>
