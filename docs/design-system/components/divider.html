Divider

Dividers are 1px lines that separate list rows, card footers and menu sections.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Prefer spacing over lines. Use dividers inside dense lists, between a card body and its footer, and between menu groups.

Anatomy

  1. Line1px --color-border-default; decorative, exempt from contrast.

Variants

or
Plain and labelled (sign-in “or”).
HTML
<hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid var(--color-border-default); width: 100%;"><div class="cluster" style="width: 100%; gap: var(--space-3);"><hr style="flex: 1; border: 0; border-top: 1px solid var(--color-border-default);"><span class="text-caption">or</span><hr style="flex: 1; border: 0; border-top: 1px solid var(--color-border-default);"></div>

Sizes

Not applicable: 1px only (<code>--border-width-hairline</code>).
