# User journeys

| # | Journey | Description | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the foundation | Scaffold backend, frontend, CI and faked e2e; build the components library, app shell, authorisation, localisation and system states. | 44 days |
| 2 | Chat with Giddy | Describe the party in chat; Giddy searches, proposes a validated plan, remembers preferences, and applies it. | 21 days |
| 3 | Start a party plan | Create a party for a child, then follow its overview, countdown and readiness from home. | 9 days |
| 4 | Find and compare venues | Search GTA venues by date, capacity and area, view details, and compare up to three. | 10 days |
| 5 | Book a venue | Hold a slot, pay the deposit, and track confirmation, cancellation and disruption status. | 14 days |
| 6 | Send invitations and track RSVPs | Design and send invitations; guests reply without an account; RSVPs, reminders and allergies update live. | 18 days |
| 7 | Build the guest list | Add, import, search, filter and remove guests, with allergy tally and undo. | 12 days |
| 8 | Find entertainers, food and cake | Search vendors by category and theme, check allergy fit, and request quotes. | 7 days |
| 9 | Choose a theme | Browse age-appropriate themes, filter them, and set one on the party with undo. | 3 days |
| 10 | Manage the budget | Track planned and paid spend against the total, and swap items when over budget. | 7 days |
| 11 | Follow the checklist | Work a generated, dated task list shared with co-planners, with daily due-task reminders. | 8 days |
| 12 | Run the party day | Follow a timed run sheet with quick calls and allergy check, even offline. | 10 days |
| 13 | Sign up and set up the family | Create and verify an account, sign in, recover access, add children, get birthday reminders. | 32 days |
| 14 | Manage plans and notifications | Share or delete plans, receive email, text and push notifications, edit settings, export or delete data. | 35 days |
| 15 | Wrap up after the party | Send thank-you notes, rate providers, and save the party as a reusable template. | 8 days |
