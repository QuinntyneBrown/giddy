# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Giddy is an agentic birthday planner for kids' parties in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). It is a responsive web application with a .NET backend and an Angular frontend. These requirements were derived from the HTML mocks in [`docs/mocks/`](../mocks/README.md) (53 screens, 217 states) and the design system in [`docs/design-system/`](../design-system/README.md). Detailed, testable requirements are in [L2.md](L2.md).

## L1-001: Accounts and authentication
The system shall let parents create an account, confirm their email address, sign in, reset a forgotten password, stay signed in across visits, and sign out, while protecting accounts against takeover.

## L1-002: Family profile
The system shall let a parent record the children they plan parties for (first name, birthday, interests and allergies or needs) and the other grown-ups who help plan, and shall use this profile to personalise suggestions and reminders.

## L1-003: Party plans
The system shall let a parent create a party plan for a child, see all their upcoming, draft and past parties, view a single party's overall readiness and status, share it with co-planners, and delete it. Once a party's date has passed, its plan shall be locked as a read-only record.

## L1-004: Planning agent ("Giddy")
The system shall provide a conversational planning agent that understands a parent's description of the party they want, checks real availability and prices for themes, venues, entertainers and food in the parent's area, proposes a complete priced plan, answers follow-up questions, and makes changes to the plan only after the parent explicitly confirms them.

## L1-005: Themes
The system shall offer a catalogue of age-appropriate party themes that parents can browse, filter and choose for a party, with suggestions that match the child's age and interests.

## L1-006: Venue discovery and comparison
The system shall let parents find GTA venues that are available on the party date and fit their guest count, budget, location and needs, view each venue's details, and compare venues side by side.

## L1-007: Venue booking
The system shall let a parent book an available venue time slot for a party, pay a deposit, receive a confirmation, and see the booking's status, including when a slot becomes unavailable or a payment fails.

## L1-008: Entertainers, activities, food and cake
The system shall let parents discover entertainers, activities, caterers and bakeries that serve the party's location and date, see allergy and dietary information for them, and request quotes.

## L1-009: Budget
The system shall track a party's budget, planned and paid costs per item, and how much is left, warn the parent when planned costs exceed the budget, and suggest ways to bring costs back within it.

## L1-010: Guest list
The system shall let a parent build and manage the party's guest list, including each child's parent contact details, RSVP status and allergies, by adding guests individually or importing them in bulk.

## L1-011: Invitations and RSVPs
The system shall let a parent design and send digital invitations by email and text message, let guests' parents reply through a private link without an account, and keep the guest list, RSVP counts and allergy list up to date in real time.

## L1-012: Checklist and reminders
The system shall give each party a dated checklist of tasks, working back from the party date, that parents can tick off, add to, and assign, with reminders before tasks are due and clear flags when they are overdue.

## L1-013: Party-day run sheet
The system shall give the parent a minute-by-minute run sheet for the day of the party, including vendor contacts and the allergy list, which keeps working without an internet connection.

## L1-014: Wrap-up after the party
After the party, the system shall help the parent send thank-you notes, rate the venue and vendors, record final numbers, and save the plan as a starting point for next time.

## L1-015: Notifications
The system shall keep parents informed of RSVPs, quotes, bookings, reminders and problems through in-app notifications, a notification centre, and email, text message and push channels, according to each parent's preferences.

## L1-016: Home dashboard
The system shall give signed-in parents a home screen showing their next party's countdown and readiness, the agent's prioritised suggestions ("nudges"), RSVP status and upcoming birthdays.

## L1-017: Settings, privacy and data rights
The system shall let parents manage their profile, notification preferences, what the agent remembers about their family, cookie consent, and their right to download or delete their data.

## L1-018: Security and data protection
The system shall protect parents' and children's personal information and payments against unauthorised access and common web attacks, and shall comply with Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).

## L1-020: Accessibility
The system shall conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA so that parents and guests using assistive technology, keyboards or larger text can complete every task.

## L1-021: Responsive design and theming
The system shall work on every viewport size from small phones to large desktops, follow the Giddy design system, and offer light and dark themes.

## L1-022: Consistent system states and error handling
Every screen shall handle loading, empty, error, offline and permission-denied situations consistently, explaining what happened and offering a way forward without losing the parent's work.

## L1-023: GTA service area and localisation
The system shall serve the Greater Toronto Area, using Canadian English, Canadian dollars with tax-inclusive prices, Canadian postal codes and the Toronto time zone.

## L1-024: Public website and help
The system shall provide a public landing page explaining Giddy, a help centre with answers and contact options, and plain-language terms and privacy information.
