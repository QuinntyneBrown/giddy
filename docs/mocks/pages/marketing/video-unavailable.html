How Giddy works

Three steps from “what should we do?” to cake time.

  1. 💬 Step 1

    Chat about your kid

    Age, what they’re into, how many friends, your budget and neighbourhood. Talk like you would to a friend.

  2. 🎈 Step 2

    Get a priced plan

    Giddy checks real availability and prices, then proposes a theme, venue, entertainer and food that fit.

  3. ✅ Step 3

    Book, invite, enjoy

    Book in a tap, send invites, track RSVPs and allergies, and run the day from one sheet.
