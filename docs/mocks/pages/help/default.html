Help & questions

Quick answers, or a real person in Toronto within one business day.

How does Giddy pick venues?

Giddy only suggests venues in your area that list the right age range, fit your guest count and are free on your date. Prices include tax so totals don’t surprise you.

Is Giddy free?

Planning is free. When you book a venue or vendor through Giddy you pay them directly; we never add a booking fee for parents.

How do allergies work?

Guests’ parents add allergies when they RSVP. Giddy flags food and venues that don’t fit and builds one list you can share with the venue.

Can my partner help plan?

Yes. Open a party and choose Share plan to invite a co-planner. They see and edit everything you do.

What areas do you cover?

Toronto and the GTA: Durham, York, Peel and Halton regions. More of Ontario is coming in 2027.
