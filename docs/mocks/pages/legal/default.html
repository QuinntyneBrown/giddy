Terms & privacy

Last updated Thu 1 Oct 2026

Giddy is built for parents planning kids’ parties in Toronto and the GTA. Here’s the short version, in plain words.

What we collect

Your name and email, your kids’ first names, birthdays and interests, and the guest details you add. Guests’ parents share their own contact details and allergies when they RSVP.

What we never do

We never sell data, never show ads to children, and never contact your guests except to deliver invitations and reminders you asked us to send.

Where it’s stored

In Canada, encrypted at rest. You can download or delete everything from Settings at any time.

Booking with venues and vendors

When you book, the venue or vendor is your contract partner. Giddy passes on only what they need: date, time, guest count and allergies.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in and remember this choice. Measurement cookies show us which pages help parents most; they load only if you allow them. We ask again after 12 months.
